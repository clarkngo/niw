§4 — Critical path

Confirm INA 245(k) before spending real money

Your wife's I‑130 likely can't carry you to a green card — INA 245(c) has no exception for spouses of LPRs (only immediate relatives of US citizens). Your entire adjustment‑of‑status strategy rests on whether 245(k)'s 180‑day exception, counted from your December 2023 F‑1 re‑entry, covers the unauthorized‑work period tied to the H‑1B revocation. Nobody with a law license has confirmed this in writing yet.

This is a threshold legal question, not a case‑strength question. Get a licensed attorney's written answer before signing any retainer — it decides whether NIW+AOS even works, or whether consular processing is the real path.
